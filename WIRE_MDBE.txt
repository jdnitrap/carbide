Carbide lives in carbide_modules/layers.py together with LayerStack.

Import:
    from carbide_modules.layers import Carbide, LayerStack, strips, STRIP_WIDTH

mdbe.py owns the 6 hex flags, grammar columns, SelectiveSSM, and MDBE.base/proj.
layers.py owns Layer 1 / 2 / 3 ingest, named strips, Block re-injection, Carbide.
trace_weights.py owns read-only labels for learned cells.
discover_dimension.py owns proposed extra columns (beside only; fix after training).

The SSM sees numbers only. Names stay on Copy A (strips / named_values).
